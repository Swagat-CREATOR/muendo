Mewndo isn't connected to its core. Lanes keep running there; they come back here when it reconnects.

New lane

A lane is a terminal Mewndo owns. An agent started here always gets what you type or say to it from Mewndo, even after it finishes.

What a lane can't do: Mewndo writes into the terminal but does not read it, so it can't tell whether the agent is waiting at a prompt. A lane ends when Mewndo quits, and only the last 256 KB of output is kept for a window that opens later.
